Three ways to rebuild this screen

All three are the same palette, square corners, and the same content — what differs is how the five settings groups are reached and how much room the preview gets. Drawn at the real 1400px the editor occupies. Nothing is built yet.

Option L1 · Retheme in place

Keep the shape, change the skin

The structure you have now — vertical settings nav on the left, preview on the right — recoloured and squared. The two panel headers ("Settings", "Live Preview") collapse into the top bar and the preview strip, which is where the vertical space comes back. Smallest change: CSS and a couple of wrappers, no React restructuring.

Option L2 · Tabs across the top

Give the settings their full width

The 196px vertical nav becomes a tab strip under the title bar. The settings column gains that width back, which matters for the Header and Document Box panels where colour, typography and spacing controls sit two-up. One component moves; the panels themselves are untouched.

Option L3 · Guided steps

Walk a first-time builder through it

Same five groups, presented as an ordered path with a Next button, and a wider preview. Experienced users can still click any step directly. Biggest change: adds step state and navigation.
